Opening of classes is drawing nearer, and St. Joseph's Academy of Malinao is looking forward to welcoming Guardians back to campus on June 8, 2026. Before the first bell rings, here are a few reminders to help everyone start the school year smoothly.

Flag ceremony is held every Monday at 7:00 AM, so Guardians are encouraged to arrive on time and in proper order. As always, Guardians are asked to bring their rosaries and prayer booklets, which remain part of daily school life. On the first day, Guardians can check their classroom doors for the posted class rolls to find their section.

Old and continuing Guardians must wear their school uniform and ID on the first day of classes. Grade 7 Guardians, Grade 11 Guardians, and transferees are exempted from this requirement. For those not yet in uniform, decent dress is expected: no crossdressing, colored hair, ripped jeans, cropped tops, revealing blouses, or skirts above the knee.

These reminders are shared to help every Guardian begin the school year ready and looking their best. St. Joseph's Academy of Malinao looks forward to seeing familiar faces and new ones alike on June 8.
