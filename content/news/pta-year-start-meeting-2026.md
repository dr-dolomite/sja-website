St. Joseph's Academy of Malinao, Aklan Inc. held its Parent-Teacher Association year-start meeting, bringing parents, teachers, and school administrators together to open School Year 2026-2027 as partners in every Guardian's growth.

The gathering walked parents through the school policies, academic programs, and student welfare matters that shape daily school life, along with a look ahead at the activities planned for the year.

Beyond the agenda, the meeting was a chance to reconnect. Parents and teachers sat together as one community, renewing the trust that carries a Josephian learner from the first day of classes to the last.

Thank you to every parent who showed up and stayed engaged. That partnership is what keeps St. Joseph's Academy a supportive, faith-centered, and nurturing place to learn.
