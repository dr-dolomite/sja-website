With profound joy and heartfelt gratitude, the St. Joseph's Academy of Malinao, Aklan Inc. family extends its warmest congratulations to our School Director, Rev. Fr. Mark Randy G. Beluso, S. Th. L., MS, MALS, on his 25th Presbyteral Anniversary. He was ordained to the priesthood on July 30, 2001.

"Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant," the Gospel of Matthew tells us. Twenty-five years of faithful priestly ministry have been a steady witness to those words: humble service, compassionate leadership, and constant dedication to the Church and to Catholic education.

Through his guidance, Guardians have been encouraged to grow not only in knowledge but in faith, integrity, and service to others. Generations of Josephians have passed through this campus formed by that example, and the school carries it forward in everything it teaches.

As the community marks this milestone, it gives thanks to God for the gift of his vocation and for the many lives his ministry has touched. May the Lord continue to bless him with abundant grace, good health, wisdom, and strength in the years of shepherding still ahead.

Congratulations on your Silver Presbyteral Anniversary, Fr. Mark.
