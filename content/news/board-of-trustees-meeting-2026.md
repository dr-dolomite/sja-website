St. Joseph's Academy of Malinao, Aklan Inc. held its Board of Trustees Meeting on June 16, 2026 at the SJA Audio-Visual Room, bringing together the institution's leaders and stakeholders around a shared commitment to the school's continued growth.

The meeting was attended by His Excellency, Most Rev. Cyril B. Villareal, D.D., Bishop of Kalibo and Chairman of the Board, together with Rev. Fr. Jose Gualberto I. Villasis, CEAP Superintendent of the Diocese of Kalibo, Rev. Fr. Mark Randy G. Beluso, SJA School Director, Rev. Fr. Jose R. Relente, Parish Priest of St. Joseph the Worker Parish of Malinao, and other members of the SJA Board of Trustees.

Trustees spent the session on matters concerning the school's direction, programs, and future initiatives, a continued sign of the Board's commitment to quality Catholic education and holistic formation for every Guardian.
