The banners are up, and with them come this year's houses. St. Joseph's Academy has unveiled six grade-level houses that will carry Guardians into the coming games, each one paired with a saint and a word that captures its spirit.

Grade 7 stands under the Fratelli, the Fearless. Grade 8 rallies behind the Acutechs, the Adroit. Grade 9 carries the Maximilians, the Magnanimous, while Grade 10 answers to the Chrysostomos, the Courageous. Grade 11 is represented by the Dimitrians, the Dazzling, and Grade 12 closes the line with the Augustinians, the August.

Beyond the names and colors, the houses give every grade level a banner to rally around and a story to build together as the games approach. The arena is set, and Josephians across all six levels are getting ready to see who rises.
